Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This website

The website has no sign-up, no cookies of its own, no analytics and no ads. Like most websites, it loads some content (such as fonts, the map and video thumbnails) from outside services, which see your IP address. Videos open on YouTube, which follows the Google Privacy Policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you, and we don't share them.

PhonoByte Shorts

PhonoByte Shorts is the private app the channel owner uses to publish PhonoByte World's own videos to YouTube. It is not offered to the public. With the owner's Google sign-in it uploads and schedules videos the owner has approved, and reads the channel's name to confirm which channel is connected. It does not access viewers' data or any other account.

The sign-in token stays on a device the owner controls and is sent only to Google. Data from Google APIs is not sold, shared or used for advertising, and its use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
